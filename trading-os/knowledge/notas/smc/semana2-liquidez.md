### 📅 Week 2: Liquidity & Liquidity Pools

**Goal**: Understand where the market is "hunting" and why.

#### 🎥 Watch:

- ICT 2022 Mentorship: Episodes 4–6
- YouTube: “Liquidity pools explained ICT” or “SMC Liquidity grab examples”

#### 📝 Learn:
